# Water Turbine

I made a water turbine designed for a small but fast river.
The motor sits inside a tubblewear container with a cut out for the bearing and then tape or hot glued.
Both the container and the bearing mount for the water will should just sit on rocks instead of making legs for them.
"this is better for the earth". I have it as a 3:1 gear ratio which should allow it to generate power on a dryer day.
